## Top 5 Priority Actions to Improve AI/Search Discoverability

### 1. Create a Comprehensive FERS Retirement Guide (Blog Content)
**Target queries:** "FERS retirement planning help," "FERS retirement guide," "how does FERS work"
**Why:** This entire query category is dominated by .gov pages and generic informational sites. A 3,000+ word authoritative guide covering FERS components, calculations, and planning strategies would fill a massive gap. This is the type of long-form educational content that AI Overviews pull from.
**Action:** Write and publish a definitive FERS guide at `/fers-retirement-planning-guide/` with schema markup (FAQPage, HowTo).

### 2. Build Dedicated "Financial Advisor for Federal Employees" Landing Page
**Target queries:** "federal employee financial advisor," "fiduciary financial planner federal employees"
**Why:** STWS ranks #2 for "federal employee financial advisor" with an advice article. PlanWell doesn't appear at all. The current `/certified-financial-planner-for-federal-employees/` page targets "financial planner" but misses "financial advisor" variations.
**Action:** Create a dedicated page at `/federal-employee-financial-advisor/` emphasizing fiduciary status, fee structure, and credentials. Include comparison content ("what to look for in a federal employee financial advisor").

### 3. Create TSP-Specific Content
**Target queries:** "TSP financial advisor," "TSP investment advice," "TSP rollover help"
**Why:** PlanWell is completely invisible for TSP queries. Competitors like tspadvisors.com and stwserve.com own this space. TSP is the most common entry point for federal employees seeking financial help.
**Action:** Publish a TSP planning page and 3-5 TSP blog posts (allocation strategies, L fund analysis, rollover considerations, TSP vs IRA comparison).

### 4. Get Listed on Key Directories and Third-Party Sites
**Target:** Appearing in AI Overviews and "best of" listicles
**Why:** Google's AI Overviews and LLM-based search engines heavily cite third-party directories and listicle content. PlanWell is missing from major aggregators.
**Must-do listings:**
- **Wealthtender.com** — Appeared in "best financial planner" results. Create/claim advisor profile.
- **SmartAsset.com** — Appeared in multiple queries. Submit for inclusion in their "best financial advisors for federal employees" articles.
- **FEBA (myfeba.org)** — Appeared in 5+ queries. Explore partnership or listing.
- **FedWeek.com** — Guest post or get profiled. Appears for advisor-intent queries.
- **NARFE.org** — Explore partnership or advertising. Major federal employee association.
- **Kitces.com** — Pitch a guest article about ChFEBC planning. High industry authority.
- **MyFederalRetirement.com** — Directory listing for financial professionals.
- **CFP Board (cfp.net)** — Ensure CFP profiles link to PlanWell.

### 5. Add Structured Data / Schema Markup Across the Site
**Target:** AI Overviews, rich snippets, enhanced SERP visibility
**Why:** AI search engines and Google's AI Overviews rely on structured data to identify authoritative sources. Schema markup signals what your content is about and improves citation likelihood.
**Action items:**
- **FAQPage schema** on all educational pages and blog posts
- **LocalBusiness / FinancialService schema** on homepage and service pages
- **Event schema** on webinar/workshop pages (critical for "free federal retirement webinar" queries)
- **Person schema** for advisors (Brennan Rhule, David Treece) with credentials
- **Review/Rating schema** if client testimonials exist
- **ProfessionalService schema** with `hasCredential` for ChFEBC, CFP designations

## Summary & Next Steps

**Current State:** PlanWell has respectable visibility (7/10 queries), dominates its niche credential (ChFEBC), and has strong workshop/webinar presence. However, it's invisible for three high-volume, high-intent query categories: FERS educational content, "financial advisor" queries, and TSP-related searches.

**Biggest Opportunity:** The "FERS retirement planning help" query space is wide open — no single advisor dominates. A comprehensive FERS guide could capture significant organic traffic and AI citations.

**Biggest Threat:** STWS consistently ranks higher for commercial-intent "financial advisor" queries. Their content strategy (advice articles, fiduciary messaging) is more aligned with how Google ranks for advisor-seeking queries.

**Quick Wins:**
1. Claim Wealthtender and SmartAsset profiles (1-2 hours of work)
2. Add Event schema to webinar pages (technical SEO, 2-3 hours)
3. Create a "Federal Employee Financial Advisor" page targeting the exact phrase (1 day)

**Medium-Term:**
4. Publish comprehensive FERS guide (1 week)
5. Create TSP content hub (2-3 weeks)
6. Pitch guest posts to FedWeek, Kitces, NARFE (ongoing)

**Long-Term:**
7. Build backlink profile through directory listings and partnerships
8. Monitor AI Overview citations monthly and adjust content strategy
9. Create comparison content ("PlanWell vs other federal retirement services")
