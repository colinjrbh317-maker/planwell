Hi {{FNAME}},

Thank you for spending three hours with us on Friday. David covered a lot of ground and Brennan fielded questions in the chat. We appreciate you sticking with us through the full session.

Here are the three things we most want you to walk away with:

**1. Your High-3 matters more than your current salary.**
If you have flexibility in your last three years of federal service, even a modest pay bump compounds into a permanent pension increase for the rest of your life. This is one of the highest-leverage decisions you can make in your final years.

**2. TSP, pension, and Social Security do not exist in a vacuum.**
The order you draw from each account directly affects your federal tax rate in retirement. Most federal employees we sit down with have real room to optimize this. Getting the sequence right in the first few years of retirement can save tens of thousands over a full retirement.

**3. FEHB is one of the most valuable benefits in the federal system, but only if you protect it.**
You must be enrolled in FEHB for five consecutive years before retirement to carry it into retirement. Miss that window and you lose it permanently. If you are inside that five-year window right now, this is worth a close look.
