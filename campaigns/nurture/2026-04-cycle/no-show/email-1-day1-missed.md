We do not share the recording, but we do want you to have the substance of what was covered. Here is a short summary of the four areas David walked through over the three hours, with Brennan answering questions in the chat throughout.

**FERS pension formula and optimization**
How your pension is actually calculated: 1 percent × High-3 × years of service, or 1.1 percent if you retire at 62 or later with 20+ years. More importantly, which levers you can pull in your final years of service, and which ones make the biggest difference to your lifetime payout.

**TSP withdrawal strategies**
Most federal employees do not realize that withdrawing TSP in the wrong order can push them into a higher federal tax bracket in retirement. The workshop covered sequencing (which accounts to draw from first), when a Roth conversion makes sense, and how Social Security timing fits into the full picture.

**FEHB and FEGLI decisions**
The five-year enrollment rule for carrying FEHB into retirement, how FEHB coordinates with Medicare at 65, and whether FEGLI is still worth keeping at age-adjusted rates. For a lot of federal employees approaching retirement, FEGLI becomes the wrong tool.

**Survivor benefits**
The default survivor benefit election is not always the right choice, and once you retire, your ability to change it is extremely limited. The workshop covered the real tradeoffs and what most couples get wrong.
