# Multi-Offer Meta Ads Strategy — April 2026 Cycle

## The Problem with Single-Offer Campaigns

Running only "Register for the webinar" ads leaves money on the table:
- Not everyone is ready for a 3-hour commitment
- Different people are at different stages of awareness
- A single offer can't address every objection
- You're competing for the same audience with the same message every cycle

## The Solution: Offer Ladder

Instead of one offer, run 3-4 offers that match different awareness levels.
Each offer feeds the next — everyone eventually gets routed to webinar registration.

```
COLD AUDIENCE                    WARM AUDIENCE                   HOT AUDIENCE
(never heard of PlanWell)        (visited site / downloaded)     (engaged, ready)

│ Free FERS   │──pixel───▶   │ Free Calculator  │──pixel─▶ │ Webinar Signup   │
│ Guide       │              │ Tool             │          │ (3-hr workshop)  │
│ (PDF lead   │              │ (use & see your  │          │                  │

```

## Offer 1: Free FERS Retirement Guide (Top of Funnel)

**What:** The existing FERS guide PDF that's already in Mailchimp
**Who:** Cold audience, interest-targeted federal employees
**Why it works:** Low commitment (just enter email), high perceived value
**CTA:** "Get Your Free Guide"
**Landing page:** Dedicated lead magnet page (or popup on homepage)
**Post-conversion:** Enters Mailchimp nurture → eventually gets webinar invite

**Ad Creative Style:**
- Mockup of the guide on a tablet/phone screen
- "The 47-page guide every fed needs before retiring"
- Carousel: show 3-4 page previews from the guide

**Ad Copy (PAS framework):**
> Retiring from federal service without a plan? The average FERS employee makes 3 costly mistakes with their pension, TSP, and FEHB. Our free 47-page guide walks you through every decision — with dollar amounts. Download it in 30 seconds.

**Budget:** $5/day from the cold-interest ad set

## Offer 2: Free Calculator Tool (Middle of Funnel)

**What:** Link to planwellfp.com/calculators (FERS pension, sick leave, TSP, FEHB)
**Who:** Warm audience (guide downloaders, site visitors), also cold
**Why it works:** Interactive, personalized, shows them THEIR numbers. No email required — just pixel them.
**CTA:** "Calculate Your Pension" or "See Your Numbers"
**Landing page:** /calculators or specific calculator page
**Post-conversion:** Pixeled → retargeted with webinar ad

**Ad Creative Style:**
- Screenshot mockup of calculator with real numbers showing
- "Your FERS pension in 60 seconds — enter your high-3 and years of service"
- Native feel — looks like someone sharing a cool tool, not an ad

**Ad Copy (Before-After-Bridge):**
> Before: "I think my pension will be around $3,000/month... maybe?"
> After: "My FERS pension is exactly $4,847/month, and I know the best date to retire."
> PlanWell's free calculator does the math in 60 seconds. No signup needed.

**Budget:** $3/day from warm-retarget ad set

---

## Offer 3: Free Webinar (Bottom of Funnel — Primary)

**What:** The 3-hour FERS Retirement Workshop
**Who:** Warm audience (guide downloaders, calculator users, site visitors), also cold
**Why it works:** Highest-intent action, leads directly to 1-on-1 call bookings
**CTA:** "Save Your Seat" or "Register Free"
**Landing page:** /webinar

**Ad Creative Style (v2 — not boring):**
- Lifestyle shot: person at kitchen table watching webinar on laptop
- Before/after: "confused" vs "confident" split screen
- Video: 30-sec clip of Brennan/David answering a common question
- Carousel: "5 things you'll learn in our free workshop"

**Ad Copy (Social Proof Lead):**
> "This workshop changed my entire retirement plan." — GS-14, DoD
>
> Over 10,000 federal employees have attended our free FERS workshop. In 3 hours, you'll learn your exact pension number, optimal retirement date, and TSP withdrawal strategy.
>
> Next session: [DATE]. Save your seat →

**Budget:** $10/day split across all ad sets

## Offer 4 (Future): Retirement Readiness Quiz (Viral Top of Funnel)

**What:** Interactive quiz: "How Retirement-Ready Are You?" — 8-10 questions
**Who:** Broadest cold audience
**Why it works:** Quizzes have 2-3x higher completion than static lead magnets. Captures email + segments by score (Not Ready / Getting Close / Almost There).
**CTA:** "Take the Quiz"
**Post-conversion:** Score-based Mailchimp tag → personalized nurture sequence

**Status:** Not built yet. Would need:
- Quiz page on planwellfp.com (or Typeform/Outgrow embed)
- 8-10 questions about retirement timeline, TSP balance, pension knowledge, FEHB understanding
- Score tiers with personalized results + CTA to webinar
- Mailchimp integration to tag by score

**Ad Creative Style:**
- "Most federal employees score below 60%. How ready are you?"
- Interactive-looking mockup with progress bar
- Native quiz-result format (like BuzzFeed style)

**Budget:** $5/day (when built)

## Creative Matrix (What to Test)

### Round 1 (Week 1-2): Test creative concepts
| Creative | Offer | Audience | What We're Testing |
|----------|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Concerned Fed (human) | Webinar | Warm-Retarget | Human face vs graphic |
| Calculator Screenshot | Calculator | Cold-Interest | Native feel vs branded |
| Before/After Split | Webinar | Cold-Interest | Contrast hook vs stat hook |
| Lifestyle Kitchen | Webinar | Warm-Email | Aspirational vs fear-based |
| Guide Mockup | FERS Guide | Cold-Interest | Lead magnet vs direct webinar |
| Stat-Callout (v1) | Webinar | Cold-Lookalike | Branded graphic baseline |

### Round 2 (Week 2-3): Double down on winners
- Kill anything with CPR > $25 or CTR < 0.8%
- Move budget from losers to winners
- Generate 2 new variants of the winning concept

### Round 3 (Week 3-4): Urgency phase
- Switch all copy to urgency/scarcity
- Add countdown overlays to winning creatives
- Increase retarget budget, decrease cold budget

## Video Ad Strategy (Future — When Podcast Starts)

**30-Second Script Template:**

```
[0-3 sec] HOOK: "Federal employees — stop. The #1 FERS mistake costs $127,000."
[3-8 sec] PROBLEM: "Most feds don't know their exact pension number,
          their optimal retirement date, or how to avoid the TSP tax trap."
[8-20 sec] SOLUTION: "In our free 3-hour workshop, we break down every
           number — YOUR pension, YOUR TSP strategy, YOUR FEHB options."
[20-25 sec] PROOF: "Over 10,000 feds have attended. Here's what one said:
            [cut to testimonial clip]"
[25-30 sec] CTA: "Register free at planwellfp.com/webinar. Next session: [DATE]."
```

**Video Production:**
- Record Brennan or David answering ONE question (2 min raw → edit to 30 sec)
- Use podcast episodes when available (cut the best 30-sec clips)
- Captions mandatory (85% watch without sound)
- Vertical (9:16) for Stories/Reels, square (1:1) for feed

## Key Insight from paid-ads Skill

> "Creative Testing Hierarchy: 1. Concept/angle (biggest impact) 2. Hook/headline 3. Visual style 4. Body copy 5. CTA"

We were testing at level 3 (visual style — navy box vs different navy box). The v2 creatives test at level 1 (concept: human vs calculator vs before-after vs lifestyle). That's where the real wins are.
